Why eXp Realty

A model built for agents who want ownership.

High-level facts from eXp’s published materials. Always confirm current details on official pages before you join.

Commission & capping

In general, agents retain an 80/20 split until reaching a $16,000 annual company-dollar cap, then keep 100% of commission for the rest of the anniversary year (subject to standard transaction fees). Mentor-program agents may see an additional split on early transactions. Income disclosure

Clear brokerage fees

U.S. agents: $149 one-time startup and $85/month cloud brokerage fee, plus published per-transaction fees. See eXp’s join FAQ and income page for the current schedule. Join overview

Revenue share & equity

Sponsor productive agents and eXp may share company dollar back through Revenue Share — performance-based, not a salary. Equity pathways include production awards and the ICON program (up to $16,000 in stock awards with vesting), plus an optional Agent Equity Program for discounted EXPI purchases.

Tech, training & community

Cloud brokerage tools (My eXp, SkySlope, eXp World, The HUB, and more), eXp University, mentoring for newer agents, and a global agent community — without traditional office walls. eXp University

How joining works

Five steps from interest to activation.

An active real estate license is required to join as an agent. eXp’s U.S. onboarding toolkit walks you through each stage.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Create your account and submit the official join application. Prefer to align sponsorship first? Talk with PurpleMtn, then apply naming our team / Monica Graves as sponsor.

  2. 2

    Onboarding review

    eXp’s Onboarding Team reviews your application.

  3. 3

    License transfer

    Transfer your license through your state board or commission, guided by eXp’s License Transfer app and your state Broker Team. Requirements are state-specific — follow official instructions; we do not give legal advice here.

  4. 4

    Board / MLS & training

    Complete required association, MLS, and training items for your market.

  5. 5

    Activation

    Your account activates with full access. Track status anytime in My eXp.
